Westworth Is Watching
A volunteer group of Westworth Village residents. Not affiliated with the City of Westworth Village, and not an official city website. Not affiliated with any campaign.
Everything on this site is drawn from public records, published documents, and things we witnessed ourselves — except where clearly labeled as opinion or speculation. If we got something wrong, tell us and we will correct it: reach us through the sign-request form, or talk to the neighbor who delivered your sign.
