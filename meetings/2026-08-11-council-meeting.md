## The rezoning passed

FACT: The council approved the application from Shaddock Homes to rezone, replat, and develop 220 Redbird Lane (agenda items 7.A, the public hearing, and 7.B, the action), converting the property from single-family SF-A zoning to a gated Planned Development with SF-B underlying zoning. The application materials call the project "The Gates at Westworth Village"; the council's agenda uses only the address, so match documents by "220 Redbird Lane." This followed the [August 4 recommendation to approve](/meetings/2026-08-04-pz-meeting/) from the P&amp;Z commission, reconstituted three weeks earlier. This account comes from residents who attended; the city had not posted the August 11 minutes as of August 15.

UNVERIFIED: The vote breakdown, and what was said in the public hearing. When the minutes or video post, they go here.

FACT: For anyone considering a challenge: under Texas Local Government Code § 211.0065, a suit challenging a residential rezoning ordinance like this one must generally be filed within 60 days of the ordinance's effective date. The effective date depends on the adopted ordinance, which the city has not yet posted; we'll add the calendar deadline when it appears.

## Also that night

FACT: The July 14 minutes, the ones recording the [3 to 2 removal of three sitting P&amp;Z members](/meetings/2026-07-14-council-meeting/) with no cause stated, were on the consent agenda for approval. The agenda also carried preliminary action on a proposed FY 2026-27 tax rate of $0.475 per $100 (public hearings September 8 and September 15), a $384,501 city-hall green space contract, an ambulance-subscription ordinance, an election-services resolution, and another executive session listing "board, committee, and commission appointments."

UNVERIFIED: Outcomes of those items, pending the minutes.

FACT: Nothing anywhere on the agenda addressed the [August 4 demand letter](/files/demand-letter-pz-removal-2026-08-04.pdf), rescission of the July 14 removals, or the Open Meetings Act. The letter's ten-business-day response window runs out August 18.

## What comes next

FACT: The commission that recommended this rezoning is the same commission the demand letter says was installed in violation of the Open Meetings Act's notice requirements, and the letter put the city on notice that actions flowing from the July 14 removals are subject to challenge as products of a voidable act. Upcoming meetings: P&amp;Z September 1 at 6 PM, regular council September 8 at 7 PM, special council September 15 at 6 PM, per the posted agenda.

OPINION: The first rezoning through the rebuilt commission moved from first hearing to final approval in seven days flat, in August, with the minutes documenting the commission shake-up approved the same night on a no-discussion consent vote.
