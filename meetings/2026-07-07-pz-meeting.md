## What happened

FACT: Per the minutes (published inside the [August 4 P&amp;Z packet](https://www.cityofwestworth.com/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_08042026-197)), Madam Chair Darla Thornton presided, with commissioners Aaron Feldman, Dani Briones, Riley Swanston, and Gary Fitzjarrell present, and Jordon Collis and Russell Laughlin absent. The commission approved its June 2 minutes 5–0 and approved the Shady Oaks storm-drainage and water-metering item — conditioned on the city engineer's written final report — 5–0. The meeting adjourned at 6:22 PM. Mayor Jones and City Attorney Kenan were both present.

## Why this page exists

FACT: Nothing in these minutes records any criticism of any commissioner, any dispute, or any hint that a shake-up was coming. Seven days later, at the July 14 council meeting, Thornton, Swanston, and Collis were [removed mid-term without stated cause](/meetings/2026-07-14-council-meeting/), on an agenda that said only "appointments."

OPINION: When the record shows a commission doing routine, unanimous work one week — with the mayor watching — and a purge the next, the burden is on the council to explain what changed. It still hasn't.
