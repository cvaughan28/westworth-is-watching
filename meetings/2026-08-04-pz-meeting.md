## What was on the agenda

FACT: The posted agenda opened with "CALL TO ORDER AND INTRODUCTION OF NEW MEMBERS," followed by mid-cycle elections for chair and vice-chair — necessary because the elected chair had been [removed three weeks earlier](/meetings/2026-07-14-council-meeting/). Then: a public hearing and action on an application from **Shaddock Homes** to rezone, replat, and develop **220 Redbird Lane** ("The Gates at Westworth Village") from single-family SF-A to a gated-community Planned Development with SF-B underlying zoning, plus review of the Kings Branch drainage study for the Shady Oaks site plan.

FACT: To be precise about what this rezoning is — and isn't: The Gates is a Shaddock Homes project on Redbird Lane. It is **not** the [Kite Farm / Trinity Terrace project](/topics/trinity-terrace/); no application under that name has appeared on any posted agenda. This was, however, the first rezoning vote taken by the commission as reconstituted after the removals.

## What happened

FACT: The commission recommended approval of The Gates application. We know this from the city's own [August 11 council agenda](https://www.cityofwestworth.com/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_08112026-200), which schedules a public hearing and final council action on "the Planning and Zoning Commissions recommendation to approve" the application.

FACT: That morning, the [demand letter](/files/demand-letter-pz-removal-2026-08-04.pdf) delivered to the city had asked that the commission, as presently constituted, refrain from taking up any substantive rezoning matter until the July 14 removals are cured — and put the city on notice that actions taken by the commission are subject to challenge as products of a voidable act. The commission proceeded anyway.
