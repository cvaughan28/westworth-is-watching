## What was on the agenda

FACT: Per the posted agenda packet, the open-session items included the Shady Oaks site plan drainage study, an alley-drainage engineering contract with Carrillo Engineering (up to $23,000), and review of the FY 2026–27 budget.

FACT: The executive session list included the Burgess condemnation, the Digital Resources litigation, an ambulance policy item, and board appointments. The "legal representation of a council member in pending civil litigation" item that appeared on the June agendas was not on the list.

## Why it matters

FACT: This packet also contains the official minutes of the June 9 and June 18 meetings — the walkout, the legal-fees vote, and the P&amp;Z replacements, in the city's own words. That's why it's the first document on our [home page](/westworth-is-watching/#documents).

OPINION: A condemnation — the government taking private property — appearing only as a name on a closed-session list is exactly the kind of item this site exists to watch. Executive sessions are legal for litigation advice, but decisions and votes belong in the open part of the meeting.
