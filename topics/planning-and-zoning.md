## What P&Z does, and who controls it

FACT: The Planning &amp; Zoning Commission reviews every rezoning request in Westworth Village before it reaches the council. Under the [city's own code](https://ecode360.com/40202654), members are nominated and approved by a simple majority of the council, serve two-year terms ending in May — and the council may remove or replace any member at any time, by simple majority.

FACT: So let's be clear about something up front: the council had the legal power to do what it did. This page isn't about whether it was legal. It's about why it happened and how it was communicated.

## What happened on June 18

FACT: Per the official minutes, at the June 18 special session the council replaced Michael Bachand, Brad Turner, and Anita Gray on the commission with Aaron Feldman, Russell Laughlin, and Gary Fitzjarrell, for terms ending May 2028. The vote was 4–0 with Fitzgerald abstaining. [Our notes from that night](/westworth-is-watching/meetings/2026-06-18-special-session/), with the minutes linked.

FACT: Context from the same record: Turner had just won a council seat, a natural reason to leave P&amp;Z. Bachand had just lost his council race by 2 votes and had filed the lawsuit contesting it — the same lawsuit the council voted, minutes earlier that night, to fund the other side of.

UNVERIFIED: We're still assembling the commission's history — when each removed member was appointed, and what the commission took up at its May meetings. If you have those minutes or were in the room, help us complete the record.

## The question nobody answered

FACT: A rezoning connected to the [Trinity Terrace project](/westworth-is-watching/topics/trinity-terrace/) was previously rejected, and the project is still alive.

SPECULATION: Replacing three commissioners in one night changes who takes the first vote the next time that rezoning comes back. Whether that was the point is our speculation — but "why these three, why that night, and why did no agenda say so in plain words" are questions the council could answer in two minutes at any open meeting. So far it hasn't.

OPINION: Having the power to do something quietly is not the same as being right to do it quietly.
